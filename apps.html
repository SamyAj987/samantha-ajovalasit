WhaDSApp: Debt Sustainability Analysis dashboard Recorded demo

GRINS Foundation, Spoke 4 · University of Palermo · 2024

WhaDSApp is an advanced interactive dashboard for conducting sophisticated Debt Sustainability Analysis (DSA). Using a comprehensive set of macroeconomic factors, it helps users autonomously evaluate the sustainability of a country's debt, built for financial analysts, government officials, policymakers, journalists and academic researchers. Entirely developed in Python with open-source libraries and the property software GAMS for advanced modelling.

EUMEPLAT media history dashboard Live

EU H2020 EUMEPLAT · Ca' Foscari University of Venice · 2021–2022

How did Europeans inform and entertain themselves over the last forty years? The dashboard answers country by country and medium by medium (press, radio, TV, OTT, ICT), with choropleth maps, convergence clusters and descriptive statistics over data collected across the EUMEPLAT consortium. Built in R Shiny and published as EU H2020 deliverable D1.5.

Curious about the data behind it?

Happy to walk through the sources, the methods, and what the numbers do and do not say.
