WhatDSApp Live

Sovereign debt risk · GRINS project · 2023–2026

Problem
Economists needed to test how fiscal choices and market shocks change the risk profile of public debt, without running simulation code themselves.
What I built
A full-stack decision-support web app on top of a stochastic simulation engine (Monte Carlo, scenario and sensitivity analysis). Drag-and-drop multi-sheet Excel ingestion, market data from FactSet and Refinitiv Eikon, fan charts and CVaR efficient-frontier plots. Containerised with Docker and deployed on the AMELIA cloud platform.
Result
Adopted by GRINS consortium partners for live debt-scenario analysis; the models behind it support a peer-reviewed paper (Risks 2024) and two Bruegel publications.

EUMEPLAT Dashboard Live

Media market analytics · EU H2020 · 2021–2022

Problem
Measure how far European media markets converged over 40 years, and make the evidence explorable for a non-technical consortium.
What I built
Clustering analysis across countries and media (Press, Radio, TV, OTT, ICT) and a public interactive dashboard with choropleth maps and descriptive statistics.
Result
Published as EU H2020 deliverable D1.5, dashboard publicly available.

Judicial efficiency analytics

Just Smart project · University of Palermo · 2022–2023

Problem
Courts in western Sicily lacked a measurable view of where cases stall.
What I built
Data retrieval from the Italian judicial repository (SICID) and a pipeline measuring process efficiency, with queueing analysis and survival models; NLP on unstructured judicial documents.
Result
Bottlenecks and processing times quantified and turned into data-driven recommendations for court administration.

Social media NLP: vaccine hesitancy

PhD research · University of Messina · 2018–2020

Problem
Does contradictory political communication change public attitudes to vaccination?
What I built
A streaming collection pipeline from the Twitter API (Tweepy) and an NLP workflow for sentiment analysis and text classification on Italian tweets, with polarity time series aligned to political events.
Result
Measured disorientation in the public debate after political announcements; published in PLoS ONE (2021).
