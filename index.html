About

Economist by training, data scientist by practice: PhD in Economics, Management and Statistics, Master's in Big Data Analytics & Social Mining. The work runs from stochastic models of public debt to NLP on Italian social media and forty years of European media data, and it usually ends in an app someone else can click through.
